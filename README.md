# VERITY

### A private market for verified intelligence.

VERITY is a privacy-preserving intelligence marketplace where people are rewarded for discovering and verifying information without exposing their identity or transaction history.

The project uses STRK20 privacy primitives on Starknet to enable private bounty funding, private staking, privacy-preserving intelligence submissions, verification incentives, and private settlement.

## Status

🚧 Early development — STRK20 Hackathon 2026

## Vision

VERITY aims to create a market where people can monetize useful information while preserving the privacy of the people who discover, verify, and consume that information.
